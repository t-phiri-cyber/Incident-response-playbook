\*\*Handling requirements:\*\*

\- Preserve original evidence wherever possible.

\- Use working copies for analysis.

\- Restrict access to authorised personnel.

\- Record evidence transfers and significant handling actions.

\- Protect evidence against alteration, loss and unauthorised access.

\- Follow organisational retention and legal requirements.
