\# Incident Report – DATA-004



\## 1. Incident Identification

\- Incident reference: DATA-004

\- Incident title: Unauthorised External Disclosure of Customer Data

\- Incident category: Personal Data Breach

\- Date and time detected: 3 November 2026 – Simulation

\- Date and time reported: 3 November 2026 – Simulation

\- Incident lead: Northstar Security Incident Lead

\- Reporting team: IT Department

\- Current status: Closed in simulation



\## 2. Incident Description

An employee accidentally emailed a spreadsheet containing 250 customer records to an unauthorised external recipient.



The spreadsheet contained customer names, email addresses and dates of birth.



The recipient opened and downloaded the attachment before being contacted by the organisation.



\## 3. Severity Classification

\- Initial severity: SEV 2 – High

\- Final severity: SEV 2 – High

\- Reason: Unauthorised disclosure of 250 customer records.

\- Escalation history: Referred to the incident lead and privacy team.



\## 4. Scope and Impact

\- Affected systems: Corporate email system.

\- Affected users: 250 customers.

\- Data involved: Names, email addresses and dates of birth.

\- Business impact: Unauthorised disclosure of customer information.

\- Confirmed impact: External recipient accessed and downloaded the spreadsheet.

\- Potential impact: Identity fraud, phishing and targeted scams.

\- Unconfirmed findings: Further distribution or retention after deletion request.



\## 5. Investigation Findings

\- Root cause: Employee accidentally sent customer information to an unauthorised external recipient.

\- Email delivery: Confirmed.

\- Attachment opened: Confirmed.

\- Attachment downloaded: Confirmed.

\- Encryption: Spreadsheet was not encrypted.

\- Further distribution: No evidence identified.

\- Recipient deletion: Written confirmation received.

\- Independent verification of deletion: Not possible.

\- Investigation limitations: No independent access to the recipient's device.
